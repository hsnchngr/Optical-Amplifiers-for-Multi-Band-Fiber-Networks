Parameters: q=<string of D1-D7>; type=dataset; size=25 (all hits returned on one page). The number of hits is hits.total.
Z1 (string D1) hits 2; Z2 (D2) hits 0; Z3 (D3) hits 9; Z4 (D4) hits 2; Z5 (D5) hits 1; Z6 (D6) hits 2; Z7 (D7) hits 1

Totals: 282 DataCite hits + 17 Zenodo hits = 299 hits; 231 unique DOIs (version DOIs of one deposit counted separately). Screening decisions for every DOI: si_s1_search_log.csv.

Screening codes in si_s1_search_log.csv
I    included (criteria i-iii met)
T1   title screening: not about an optical amplifier of the three technologies, or unrelated
T2   title screening: not a data deposit (supplementary PDF, preprint, thesis, software)
T3   title screening: other device, use or band (laser, parametric or waveguide amplifier, pulsed or visible source, SOA used as switch or logic gate, outside 1260-1675 nm)
F1t  criterion (i): transmission or nonlinearity-compensation experiment; amplifier gain or noise-figure data not identified
F1s  criterion (i): simulation or model output
F1r  criterion (i): deposit supporting a review article
F1q  criterion (i): other quantities only
F1n  criterion (i): content not identifiable from the metadata
F1x  criterion (i): no data file (citation file only)
F2   criterion (ii): available on request only
F3   criterion (iii): band or wavelength range not stated in the record or in the abstract of the supported paper

Screening sources: DataCite record metadata; landing pages of five Aston University records (00000205, 00000286, 00000587, 00000589, 00000663) and of Zenodo record 14795809; abstracts of supported papers through the OpenAlex API where the record did not state the band or the measured quantity. University of Southampton landing pages returned HTTP 403 to automated requests and were not inspected. No data file was downloaded.
